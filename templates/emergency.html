🆘 What To Do During An Emergency

1
Stay calm
Keep yourself and the patient as calm as possible.
2
Call for help
Dial 112 for urgent emergency assistance.
3
Share your location
Clearly tell the emergency service where you are.
4
Follow professional instructions
Follow the instructions given by emergency responders.
